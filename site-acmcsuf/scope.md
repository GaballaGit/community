# Site Group Scope

The Site Group is responsible for the site found at **[acmcsuf.com](https://acmcsuf.com)**.

## Responsibilities

The group focuses on:
* Designing and maintining the acmcsuf site. This involves creating, styling, and maintining the various pages on the site.
* Creating and maintining frontend elements in svelte. 
* Tying in our backend services to the site.
* Designing tests for services used across the site.

## Projects

The Site group will only focus on the **[acmcsuf.com project](https://github.com/acmcsufoss/acmcsuf.com)**.

## Out of Scope

* Backend services not related to the site.
* Infrastructure work. 
* Any project that is unrelated to the ACM @ CSUF ecosystem.

# AI Policy

If you looked around at our repos, you may have noticed we do not include files to make agentic ai development easier (such as AGENTS.md, DESIGN.md, etc.), why is this?

We prioritize being able to learn from the projects that are being worked on. The greatest benifit of working on open source projects at ACM @ CSUF is that you get to work with other experienced peers, particularly the board members. When introducing better agentic flow to our projects, contributors will miss the opportunity to talk to more experienced members, study the codebase, and write the code themselves. All which are extremely valuable to learn. 

### When is it okay to use AI?

We are okay with AI usage under three conditions:
> Its to understand the codebase 
> Repeat repetative tasks
> AI code review

For AI code reviews, we still require the code to also be reviewed by a human as well.

Fully vibe coding a PR is NOT okay!!!
